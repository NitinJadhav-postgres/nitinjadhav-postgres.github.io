This commit adds a test case for early startup where a `backup_label`
file uses checkpoint and redo LSNs located in different WAL segments,
and the segment containing the redo LSN is missing.

The test uses the checkpoint and redo LSNs generated during its initial
steps together with a generated `backup_label`.
